What makes a top performer? An HR data deep dive

In todays world, most organisations 
measure employee performance but few 
understand what drives it. It is 
imperative that companies understand 
this as they lose money and talent when 
they can't identify what drives 
performance. When the gap between low and 
high performers is left unaddressed, it 
shows up in dissatisfied employees, 
reduced productivity etc. 

Using the Employee Performance and 
Productivity Dataset, this dashboard uses 
employee data to profile both ends of the 
performance spectrum to answer:

> What separates a top performer from a low performer and what does it cost a company when that gap goes unaddressed?
